Le Petit Chalet

The authentic charm of a cosy Alpine retreat. Wooden finishes, an intimate atmosphere and stunning Matterhorn views invite you to slow down and enjoy the simple pleasures of mountain life.

A home for every season in the mountains, a short distance from Breuil-Cervinia.

Designed for up to four guests, Le Petit Chalet welcomes you with cosy rooms and natural wooden details inspired by traditional Alpine chalets. Its two bedrooms offer a restful place to unwind after skiing or exploring the outdoors.

The Matterhorn takes centre stage, from the first light of morning to the colours of sunset. The apartment is located near Breuil-Cervinia, an ideal base for discovering the ski area and the Valtournenche valley.

Passo nel Cielo

In Cielo Alto, bright interiors, wood and stone details and welcoming shared spaces make the perfect setting to relax after a day on the slopes.

An apartment designed for families and small groups, with a living room, kitchen, comfortable bedrooms and true Alpine character.

Passo nel Cielo is located in Cielo Alto, near Breuil-Cervinia, and welcomes up to six guests. A sociable living area, equipped kitchen and two bedrooms make it ideal for families and groups who enjoy spending time together while keeping their own pace.

The combination of wood, stone and contemporary details creates a relaxed balance of Alpine character and everyday comfort. After a day in the snow, gather in the living room, prepare dinner and plan tomorrow's adventures.
